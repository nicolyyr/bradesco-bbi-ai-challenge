CEO:
Good morning, everyone. Welcome to our fourth quarter 2025 earnings release. This was a strong quarter to close the year, and I will walk you through results, profitability, the loan portfolio, expenses, and delinquency, before we open for the Q&A session. The headline message for the full year is consistency: we delivered record profitability with disciplined capital allocation and a resilient credit portfolio.

Beginning with results, we delivered a managerial result of BRL 11.8 billion in the fourth quarter, with strong year-over-year growth. For the full year, net income reached a record level, reflecting the durability of our business model. It is worth noting that during this quarter we made an additional dividend distribution of BRL 20 billion, which is a sign of confidence in our capital generation.

Moving on to profitability, we recorded a consolidated ROE of 24.5% and ROE in Brazil of 26.0%. These are very strong figures and reinforce our ability to create value above our cost of equity. The loan portfolio grew 1.5% in the quarter and close to 9% year-over-year excluding FX effects, with continued emphasis on target clients and high-quality originations.

On expenses, we continued to advance our efficiency program, reaching an efficiency ratio of 35.4% in Brazil, an improvement versus prior periods. We reaffirm our commitment to the efficiency targets and to the midpoint of our cost guidance, which implies annual expense growth around 3.5%.

When we look at delinquency, short-term and long-term indicators remained well-behaved. NPL ratios were broadly stable, reflecting the resilience and quality of our portfolio. Credit quality remains a central topic, and we continue to manage the portfolio with discipline, focusing on resilient target clients across the credit cycle.

On capital, we ended the quarter with a CET1 ratio above our reference level even after the significant dividend distribution. Our core capital generation comfortably funds both capital uses and the growth of risk-weighted assets. We start the next year with a solid capital base.

To conclude, this was a record year delivered with consistency and low volatility. We remain comfortable with the guidance we have set, and we are confident in our ability to keep creating value. The macro environment requires attention, but our portfolio is well positioned. I will now join the team for our Q&A session. Thank you.
