## Scenario
Scenario characterized by higher interest rates, persistent inflation, weaker growth expectations, slowing consumer spending, tighter credit conditions.

## Top Benefited Sectors
- **Banks**: Higher interest rates may support net interest income and credit spreads for large banks.
- **Insurance**: Insurers can benefit from higher yields on financial reserves and investment portfolios.
- **Utilities**: Defensive sectors may attract investors during periods of weaker growth and higher uncertainty.
- **Oil & Gas**: Companies with strong cash generation may be relatively resilient in risk-off environments.
- **Pulp & Paper**: Export-oriented companies may benefit from currency depreciation associated with weaker domestic growth.

## Top Negatively Impacted Sectors
- **Construction**: Higher interest rates increase financing costs and reduce housing affordability.
- **Retail**: Retailers are pressured by tighter credit conditions and weaker consumer demand.
- **Consumer Goods**: Persistent inflation can pressure margins and reduce household purchasing power.
- **Consumer Discretionary**: Slower consumer spending directly affects discretionary purchases and services.
- **Capital Goods**: Lower growth expectations may reduce corporate investment and demand for capital equipment.

- Inflation surprises to the downside, reducing pressure on consumer names.
